01 — About

I like the messy middle where research becomes something people can use.

I'm Chhatrapathi Sivaji — an ML engineer and Data Scientist based in New York City. I've spent the last five years working at the boundary between research and production: building risk pipelines that protected $4.5M in credit decisions at Tiger Analytics, designing LLM evaluation frameworks that cut latency by 39% at Handshake AI, and training multimodal models across audio, EEG, vision, and structured data at NYU. The common thread is turning rigorous, research-grade work into systems that actually hold up when real users hit them.
